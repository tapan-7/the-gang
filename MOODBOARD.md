# MOODBOARD — THE GANG

## Visual Identity & Emotional Atmosphere

The visual aesthetic of **THE GANG** captures the feeling of stumbling upon a cherished physical scrapbook in a sunlit attic, opening its worn cover, and watching the photographs, sketches, and scribbled notes spring to life into a vibrant, animated 2D documentary.

```text
       ┌────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
```

## 1. Materiality & Textures (The Paper Alchemy)
- **Base Surfaces**: Warm deckled-edge heavy-weight parchment (`#F3EBDD`) with subtle grain fibers and age-toned kraft paper (`#E8DDC9`).
- **Photographic Keepsakes**: Glossy-matte Polaroid prints with authentic cream border margins, light corner creases, and subtle organic tilt angles (-4° to +4°).
- **Adhesives & Binding**: Semi-transparent textured washi tape in pastel tones, brass paperclips, and red corner photo mount tabs.
- **Ephemera**: Rubber stamps with inked dates, torn notebook paper with ruled faint blue lines, coffee cup ring stains, and doodles in dark ink.

### Contextual Scene Palettes:
- **Default Scrapbook Spread**: Warm parchment background, dark ink text, terracotta and mustard tape accents.
- **Chapter 03 (Gopalpur Twilight to Sunrise)**: Transitions from sunny golden hour (`#E8A87C`) to deep twilight indigo (`#1E2A38`) with glowing fairy lights (`#FDE68A`), culminating in soft pastel morning sunrise pinks (`#FBCFE8`) and pale gold (`#FEF08A`).
- **Chapter 07 (Koraput Mountain Trek)**: Earthy moss greens (`#4D6A53`), terracotta dust trails (`#A0522D`), and crisp high-altitude sky blues (`#BEE3F8`).

---

## 3. Typographic Voice
- **The Voice of Memory (Display)**: **Fraunces** — Rich, variable serif font with soft optical curves, conveying warmth, editorial distinction, and nostalgia.
- **The Voice of the Group (Handwritten Annotations)**: **Caveat** — Natural, organic handwriting for photo captions, playful arrows, scribbles, and inside jokes.
- **The Voice of Clarity (Body)**: **DM Sans** — Clean, legible modern grotesque sans-serif ensuring effortless readability across screens.

---

## 4. Character Design Tone
- **Form Language**: Expressive, hand-drawn vector art with charcoal outlines (2-3px) and textured flat fills.
- **Proportions**: Stylized semi-realistic storybook proportions; warm, recognizable silhouettes rather than hyper-exaggerated cartoon deformities.
- **Signature Vignettes**:
  - Dushmanta frowning playfully at an endless receipt while holding a calculator.
  - Paresh with an apron and a fountain pen dipped in ink.
  - Shyamalendu passionately gesturing at a multi-branched whiteboard explanation.
  - Astha marching ahead with a clipboard and rolls of tape.
  - Asutosh gently cradling a single eggplant with zen-like serenity.
  - Ashutosh daydreaming inside a soft illustrated cloud over a feast table.
  - Arpita smiling warmly with tiffin boxes surrounded by heart stickers and dance ribbons.
  - Tapan peacefully fading slightly into the background holding a basketball and a snack.

---

## 5. Layout Philosophy: Asymmetry & Rhythm
- Avoid static corporate grids and repetitive card containers.
- Embrace dynamic, deliberate asymmetry: photos overlapping at gentle angles, notes peeking from behind frames, and trails connecting vignettes.
- Generous white space (breathing parchment) alternating with dense, chaotic photo walls.
- Every viewport tells a specific story beat.
