# PROJECT MASTER BRIEF — THE GANG
## An Illustrated, Cinematic, Interactive Digital Memory Book

**Project name:** THE GANG  
**Core tagline:** A little chaos. A lot of memories.  
**Alternative tagline:** We came for work. We stayed for each other.  
**Project type:** Personal friendship storytelling website / digital scrapbook / interactive illustrated documentary  
**Primary goal:** Preserve the memories, personalities, friendships, adventures, inside jokes, and emotional moments of our group through an immersive, beautifully designed website.

# 2. PROJECT BACKGROUND

This website documents a close-knit group of people who initially met through work at Hyscaler.

What started as a group of colleagues gradually became a group of friends.

Initially, there were only a few people. Over time, more people joined. We started having small outings, celebrations, lunches, trips, conversations, and countless random moments.

However, the group did not immediately become close.

In the beginning, things were sometimes quiet, awkward, and divided into smaller circles.

Then came the annual office retreat at Gopalpur.

That retreat changed the dynamic.

We spent time together, played games, danced, talked late into the night, shared ghost stories, played UNO, watched the sunrise, and started connecting with each other in a different way.

After that, the group became much closer.

We started celebrating birthdays, arranging outings, watching movies, having Sunday feasts, cooking together, joining cafeteria tables, and spending time with each other outside work.

Eventually, we formed a WhatsApp group called **UNO-2026**.

People have changed roles, moved on, left the company, or are preparing for new chapters in life. But those changes do not erase what we experienced together.

This website is a way of preserving those memories.

### The emotional foundation

The story is not about a company.

It is about the people we met because of a company.

It should communicate:

- We started as colleagues.
- We slowly became friends.
- We created our own little world.
- We developed our own language, jokes, traditions, and memories.
- Life keeps moving.
- People take different paths.
- But the memories remain.

### Emotional balance

Target approximately:

- 70% funny, playful, chaotic, and entertaining.
- 30% emotional, nostalgic, warm, and heartfelt.

Do not make the entire website sad or overly sentimental.

The emotional moments should feel earned through the story.

# 3. CREATIVE DIRECTION

## The central visual concept

**A physical scrapbook transforms into a cinematic illustrated world as the visitor scrolls.**

Imagine opening an old scrapbook.

The first page has a title written by hand, some photographs, paper textures, doodles, stickers, handwritten notes, and small illustrations.

As the visitor scrolls:

- A photograph slides out of a paper frame.
- A character illustration enters the scene.
- A handwritten annotation appears.
- A small doodle becomes animated.
- A scrapbook page unfolds.
- The paper background transforms into an illustrated environment.
- Characters appear together.
- Memories become short animated scenes.
- The story transitions from one chapter to another.

The website should not feel like separate sections stacked vertically.

It should feel like one continuous visual journey.

## Combine two visual styles

### A. Digital scrapbook

Use:

- Warm paper backgrounds.
- Torn paper edges.
- Polaroid photographs.
- Film strips.
- Photo corners.
- Tape and stickers.
- Handwritten annotations.
- Paper cutouts.
- Notebook pages.
- Scrapbook labels.
- Doodles and small illustrations.
- Old photographs.
- Hand-drawn arrows.
- Stamps and dates.
- Layered paper compositions.
- Imperfect but intentional layouts.

The design should feel tactile, like someone physically created this memory book.

### B. Cinematic 2D storytelling

Use:

- Hand-drawn characters.
- Illustrated backgrounds.
- Parallax environments.
- Scene transitions.
- Character entrances.
- Animated environmental details.
- Camera-like movement.
- Scroll-controlled illustrations.
- Layered SVG artwork.
- Carefully timed motion.
- Illustrated storytelling sequences.

The two styles should blend naturally.

**The scrapbook is the physical interface. The illustrated world is the story inside it.**
